flust

in development

flust is a free app to help people quit pornography, including pornography made with AI. It is in development.

What it does

flust helps you keep going over time with a day counter, ranks to unlock, a daily check-in and a journal encrypted on your phone. When an urge comes, an emergency screen helps you let it pass. A filter for explicit content, including content made with AI, will come in a later version.

What it does not do

flust has no account and sends no data anywhere. Everything stays on your phone, encrypted. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that sends data. In this version, the Android app does not even have permission to use the internet, which anyone can check. The filter planned for a later version will need it only to pass on the phone's web address lookups (DNS), never your data. Details are in the privacy policy.

The code is open source and published on github.com/fnyx-group/flust.
