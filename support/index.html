Support

For help with flust or this website, write to contact@fnyx-group.org, in English or French.

Your data is only on your phone, so we cannot access or restore it. Please do not send journal entries by email.

Security issues

Please do not report security issues in public. Email security@fnyx-group.org or use GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the flust repository. The full policy is in SECURITY.md, and the contact details are also in security.txt.

Bugs and suggestions

Other bugs and suggestions can go to the flust issue tracker on GitHub.
