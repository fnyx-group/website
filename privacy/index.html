Privacy policy

This page explains what data the flust app and the fnyx-group.org website handle.

The flust app

flust collects no data. What you enter (journal, progress, settings) stays on your phone, encrypted. There is no account. The app has no analytics, no ads and no code from other companies that sends data.

Since nothing leaves your phone, we cannot see, recover or delete your data.

This website and email

fnyx-group.org sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when you visit (see the GitHub General Privacy Statement).

Emails sent to contact@fnyx-group.org and security@fnyx-group.org are handled by Proton AG in Switzerland (see the Proton privacy policy). Messages are kept as long as needed to reply and follow up, then deleted.

Questions about this policy can go to contact@fnyx-group.org.
