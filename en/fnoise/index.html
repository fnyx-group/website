fnoise

planned

fnoise is a free app to quiet the noise in your head and on your phone. It will come after flust.

What it will do

You write down whatever keeps going round in your head, then sort each thought. The ones you cannot deal with now wait for a daily appointment you choose. When it gets too much, a screen helps you switch off with guided breathing, grounding and lines you wrote for yourself. A check-in that takes ten seconds gives you your noise level for the day, with no score and no judgment. Later, fnoise will also quiet the phone with calm hours and a pause before you open a social app.

How we are preparing it

Before any code is written, a few people will test a prototype and a psychologist will review it. fnoise does not replace medical or psychological care.

What it will not do

Like flust, fnoise will need no account and will keep everything on your phone, encrypted. There will be no ads, no analytics, no chatbot and no pushy notifications. The code will be open source.
