The association

fnyx-group is a non-profit association under the French law of 1901. It runs the development of flust and fnoise, and makes sure their code stays free. Its members are volunteers and nobody is paid.

Official details

The association was registered with the Paris police prefecture on 18 November 2026, under RNA number W751287070. Its creation was published in the French Official Journal of associations on 24 November 2026 (notice no. 2601). It is listed in the SIRENE business register under SIREN number 133 310 813.

Its registered office is at 173 rue de Courcelles, 75017 Paris.
