flust

in development

flust is a free app to help people quit pornography. It is in development.

What it does

flust lets you track your recovery over time and keep a journal. The journal is encrypted on your phone. There is an emergency button for difficult moments, and flust can filter explicit content.

What it does not do

flust has no account and sends no data anywhere. Everything stays on your phone, encrypted. It has no ads, no analytics and no code from other companies that sends data. Details are in the privacy policy.

The code is open source: github.com/fnyx-group/flust.
